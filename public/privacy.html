Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

This privacy policy explains what information Perfect Racket collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what your rights are. We try to keep this short and readable. If you have a question that isn't answered here, contact us at the email at the bottom of the page.

What we collect

Information you provide

When you take the Perfect Racket quiz, we collect the answers you give us. That includes your name, email address, age range, NTRP level, grip size, current racket, budget, play style, play frequency, swing speed, priorities, goals, pain history, injury history, and string preferences. We also store the racket, string, and tension recommendations we generate for you.

Information collected automatically

We use Plausible Analytics to understand how people use the site. Plausible is a privacy-focused analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. It records aggregated information like the country your visit came from, the device type you used, and which pages you viewed. It does not track you across sites and does not build a profile on you.

Our forms are hosted by Netlify, which receives your submission and may log the IP address it was sent from for spam prevention.

Why we collect it

  • To generate your personalized racket, string, and tension recommendations.
  • To improve the algorithm by understanding which recommendations resonate.
  • To occasionally email you with product updates, new features, or follow-up questions if you opt in.
  • To prevent spam and abuse.

Who we share it with

Perfect Racket does not sell your personal information to anyone. We share information with the following third parties only as needed to operate the service:

  • Netlify hosts the site and receives your quiz submissions.
  • Plausible Analytics receives anonymous usage data.
  • Tennis Express is our affiliate partner. When you click a recommendation link, you are taken to Tennis Express's website. Tennis Express may set their own cookies and tracking. We may receive a commission if you make a purchase. We do not share your name or email with Tennis Express unless you choose to provide it to them directly.
  • Email service providers (such as Kit or similar) may receive your name and email if you opt into our email list.

How long we keep it

Quiz submission data is retained indefinitely so we can improve the algorithm over time. If you ask us to delete your information, we will. See your rights below.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the information we have about you.
  • Request that we correct or update your information.
  • Request that we delete your information.
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time.
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell personal information, but if you live in California you have this right by default).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address at the bottom of the page. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Cookies

Perfect Racket itself does not set tracking cookies. Plausible Analytics, the analytics service we use, does not use cookies. Tennis Express, when you click through to their site, may set cookies, including a discount code cookie tied to our affiliate relationship. Those cookies are controlled by Tennis Express and governed by their privacy policy.

Children

Perfect Racket is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of the page. Significant changes will be announced on the homepage or by email if you have one on file with us.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or want to exercise any of the rights above, email us at hello@perfectracket.com.

This policy is provided for informational purposes and is not legal advice. If you have specific questions about your rights, consult a lawyer.
